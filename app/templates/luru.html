Select an exam and a class, then click Load List
How to use
1. Download the template: the Excel file lists exam numbers, names, classes and one column per subject.
2. Fill in the scores in Excel and save the file.
3. Click Upload grades and pick the file; rows are matched by exam number.
4. Blank cells are treated as absent and excluded from the statistics.
